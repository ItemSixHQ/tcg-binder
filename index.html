Terms & Conditions

The short version: this is a personal binder, not a store. Here’s exactly how it works.

No direct purchasing

This website has no direct purchasing capability — and that’s not a flaw, it’s designed this way on purpose. Jackson isn’t running an official business here; this simply serves as a digital binder so you can see what he currently has for sale.

How to buy something

Found something you want? Kindly click “Add to Poké Mart Cart” on the listing. Once you’re done browsing, go to your Poké Mart Cart and select “Contact Jack.” You can contact Jack via Telegram DMs, or by joining his Discord server and either DM-ing him privately or asking in the Pokémon text channel.

Once your message comes through, Jack will respond as soon as he can. Payment is made via PayNow or PayLah! — Jack will send over a QR code along with your final order amount and item list before you pay.

Jack has a day job and a young kid at home, so please be a little forgiving if he takes a bit longer than usual to reply — he’ll always get back to you.

Collection & mailing

Jack is based in Singapore, and can only hand over cards locally — there’s no international shipping at the moment. Once payment is confirmed, cards can be collected in person or mailed out:

  • Tampines MRT — specific days and timings only, DM Jack to discuss.
  • Upper Thomson MRT — Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, 2–7pm.
  • Mailing (SmartPac) — available for an additional $3. Multiple cards can share one SmartPac unless the order is too large.

Sending your list

The entire list of your cart, and its hyperlinks, should automatically be copied when you hit “Contact Jack” — simply paste it into the chat and he’ll respond as soon as he can.

Condition guide

Every card is graded honestly on this scale. Anything Moderately Played or below gets a written note under the listing explaining exactly where and how it’s worn.

M
Mint — flawless, pack-fresh.
NM
Near Mint — minimal to no wear.
LP
Lightly Played — minor edge/corner wear.
MP
Moderately Played — visible scratches or whitening.
HP
Heavily Played — heavy wear, possible small tears.
DMG
Damaged — bends, tears, writing, or similar.

Card flags explained

Some listings carry small badges for printing quirks that collectors look for. They’re not damage — they describe how the card came out of the factory.

Swirls
A galaxy-like swirl pattern in the holo foil, created during printing. Often sought after.
Holo Bleed
Holo foil extending slightly past the artwork window onto the border. A printing quirk some collectors prize.
Misprint
A factory error — miscut edges, off-centre printing, or ink irregularities.

About the photos

Thumbnail photos on this site are not an actual photo of the specific physical card and don’t represent its real condition. Please trust the condition, remarks, and any flags (swirls, holo bleed, misprint) stated on each individual listing. If you’d like real photos or more explanation about a specific card before committing, just message Jack — he’s happy to help.
